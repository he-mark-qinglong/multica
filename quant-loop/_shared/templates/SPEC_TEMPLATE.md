## Falsification

A strategy is killed (cancel, mark `PROFITABLE = False`) when any of
the following holds on its **out-of-sample** walk-forward fold:

- `sharpe_daily < 0` (point estimate, not just CI includes 0).
- `fee-shock 60 bps` (re-run with `cost_bps_rt = 60`) produces
  `annualized_return <= 0` or `profit_factor < 1.0`.
- `max_drawdown_pct > 20` over any contiguous OOS window.
- Regime flip (Markov-switching HMM assigns probability > 0.6 to a
  different regime than the training fold) wipes > 2× the average
  trade pnl in the next 20-bar window.

Pick the rule that fits your edge. Concrete thresholds, not
"performance degrades".

Precheck before writing any signal code:

1. Confirm at least 2 years of in-sample bars AND ≥ 1 OOS year for
   walk-forward (per `_shared.validation.cpcv` schedule).
2. Confirm the cost budget: estimated **gross edge per trade** must
   exceed the ratified **22 bps RT cost** by > 2×. If it does not,
   the strategy is structurally unfunded — kill before coding further.
3. If the hypothesis needs funding or trades: pin the data window
   inside the post-2021-11-20 / post-2026-01-01 sub-periods. Do not
   mix pre/post regimes.

## Cost constraints

Round-trip cost follows the **ratified SMA-34900 perp convention**:

- Venue: `_shared.execution.cost_model.apply_cost(venue=BINANCE_FUTURES)`.
- Per-side: 4 bps taker fee + 7 bps fixed pure slippage = 11 bps.
- Round-trip: 2 × per-side = **22 bps**, independent of trade size.

Strategies MUST source the cost via `apply_cost(venue=BINANCE_FUTURES)`
only. Inline numeric cost literals (`0.0004`, `0.0008`, `0.0011`,
`0.0016`, `0.0022`, `0.0024`, or any `fee_bps_per_fill = …`,
`FEE_RT_BPS = …`, `slippage_bps_per_side = …` assignment) are a
drift source and will be flagged by
`_shared.execution.check_inline_costs.check_inline_costs --enforce`.

Fee-shock sensitivity runs pass an explicit multiplier against the
ratified constant (e.g. `cost_bps_rt = 60`) — never inline the literal.

## Visualization & human-reviewable evidence

Every strategy MUST ship the following images in `results/` before any
KEEP/KILL verdict. Abstract metrics are not sufficient; smark and
signoff-proxy must be able to inspect individual trades.

Required artifacts (generated via `_shared.visualization.StrategyVisualizer`):

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `equity_curve.png` | Combined + long-only + short-only NAV curves with drawdown bands |
| `trade_history_long.png` | Representative long trades overlaid on K-line chart |
| `trade_history_short.png` | Representative short trades overlaid on K-line chart |
| `trade_diagnostic.png` | PnL vs hold time, distribution, hourly mean, top contributors/detractors |
| `returns_heatmap.png` | Monthly returns heatmap |
| `vpvr_overlay.png` | (if VPVR-based) volume profile with entry/exit clusters |
| `indicator_overlay.png` | (if signal uses indicator) price + indicator + trades |

Rules:

1. `trade_history_*.png` must show ≥50 representative trades spanning at
   least 3 distinct market regimes (trend up, trend down, chop).
2. Each trade must be visually traceable: entry marker, exit marker,
   hold-time connecting line, and side.
3. The researcher comment MUST explain the 3 largest drawdown periods
   and the 5 largest contributing trades, referencing the visual artifacts.
4. Missing any mandatory image = attestation fails automatically.
5. If the visual evidence contradicts the numeric summary, visual
   evidence wins and the issue is routed back for bug investigation.
